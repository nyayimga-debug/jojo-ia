Horst schraubt : le guide de l'outil

Ce guide décrit tout ce dont l'outil a besoin pour fabriquer une vidéo de Horst : quelle vidéo faire, de quelle vidéo s'inspirer, comment écrire le script, comment faire les plans de Horst, les B-rolls par IA et les B-rolls gratuits (Pixabay, Pexels…), le montage et la publication. Les mêmes informations sont dans le fichier horst_projet.json, intégré à cette page.

Les vidéos sont en allemand. Les prompts d'images et les recherches de B-roll sont en anglais, parce que les outils et les banques d'images répondent mieux en anglais.

Ce que l'outil produit pour chaque vidéo

Build a reusable tool that turns one video topic from the list below into a finished 14–20 min YouTube video: German script, one voice, lip-synced Horst clips, B-roll from AI and from free stock libraries, text cards, subtitles, final edit, and the publishing pack (title, description, chapters, tags, pinned comment, thumbnail).

Les 30 vidéos à créer (dans l'ordre de publication)

Chaque titre reprend une vidéo qui a fait beaucoup de vues. Le lien « S'inspirer de » mène à la vidéo de référence : l'outil en récupère la transcription, garde la structure et la promesse, n'en copie jamais les phrases, et ajoute ce qui manque pour l'Allemagne. La phrase en italique est la première phrase de Horst. Take the title of a video that got many views and translate it word for word into German; change only the subject to bring it to Germany. Replace any false or exaggerated word with a true one. All 30 titles below already follow this rule.

Horst, la chaîne et la voix

Chaîne

Horst schraubt · @HorstSchraubt

Public

German drivers aged 35–70 who drive used cars (VW, Mercedes, BMW, Opel, Ford, Skoda, Audi) and want to avoid expensive repairs.

Rythme

3 videos per week for the first 4 weeks (see publishing_order), then at least 2 per week.

Biographie

Horst ran a small independent car workshop (freie Werkstatt) in Bochum, in the Ruhr area, for almost 40 years. Retired, he still spends his mornings in his workshop with his red 1972 Opel Kadett on the lift. Calm, direct, dry Ruhr humour. Explains what really wears out a car and what you can do yourself. Dislikes workshop rip-offs and 'miracle tricks' from the internet.

Gags récurrents
  • His red 1972 Opel Kadett that always starts.
  • His wife Gisela, who drives an automatic and asks him questions.
  • His grandson Jonas, who wants an electric car.
Ce que Horst ne dit jamais
  • Never says he is a certified KFZ-Meister or that his workshop can be visited.
  • Never gives a fake address, fake customer reviews or fake certificates.
  • Customer stories are character stories: keep them plausible, never present them as verifiable facts or statistics.
Sources qu'il cite

ADAC, TÜV-Report, DEKRA, Manufacturer manuals (Betriebsanleitung), StVO / Bußgeldkatalog for legal points

Phrases fixes (à reprendre mot pour mot)

opening

Glück auf, ich bin Horst. Fast vierzig Jahre hab ich in meiner Werkstatt geschraubt – und heute erklär ich dir, was dir die Werkstatt meistens nicht sagt.

signature

Ich sach dir das, wie’s ist.

ending

Fahr vorsichtig, pass auf dein Auto auf – und wir sehen uns beim nächsten Mal in der Werkstatt.

ai_label_on_screen

Horst ist eine KI-Figur. Inhalte recherchiert, kein Ersatz für die Fachwerkstatt.

call_to_action

Schreib mir in die Kommentare, was für ein Auto du fährst – dann weiß ich, worüber ich als Nächstes rede.

Voix

German man, about 65–70, deep slightly husky voice, calm pace, warm and direct. Standard German with a light Ruhr colour ('sach' instead of 'sag', an occasional 'wat'), never a caricature, always easy to understand.

Use the same voice ID for every video. Speaking rate ~130–140 words per minute. Generate audio per segment so each segment can be re-done alone.

  • Write numbers as words when they are spoken in a special way: 'fünfhunderttausend Kilometer'.
  • Write abbreviations the way they are said: 'TÜV' (Tüff), 'ADAC' (A-De-A-Ze), 'DSG' (De-Es-Ge), 'DPF' → say 'Partikelfilter'.

Règles des plans

  • Horst (any HORST_* shot) is on screen 25–35 % of the runtime; B-roll and text cards fill the rest.
  • One lip-synced avatar clip is never longer than 15 s. Longer Horst passages are split and alternated with B-roll.
  • Change the picture every 5–10 s (new B-roll clip, cut to Horst, text card). Never hold one stock clip longer than 8 s.
  • First 30 seconds: Horst on screen for the first sentence (hook), then fast B-roll showing the problem, then back to Horst for the promise.
  • Each list item: TEXT_CARD with the number → Horst says the item in one sentence → B-roll while he explains → Horst comes back for the tip or the punchline.
  • Cover any glitchy lip-sync moment with B-roll; the voice continues underneath.
  • Keep the AI label on screen for the first 5 seconds of every video.

La structure d'une vidéo

Durée : 14–20 min (≈1,900–2,700 spoken German words). Never under 10 min.

PartieTempsContenu
Hook0:00–0:30Opening line of the reference title's promise in Horst's words, a concrete problem the viewer recognises (cost, breakdown, winter), and the promise of what he will show. Mention that number 1 / the last item is the most important (open loop).
Intro0:30–1:00Fixed opening line ('Glück auf, ich bin Horst…'), one sentence of credibility (40 years of workshop), one running-gag touch, then straight into item 1. No 'before we start, subscribe'.
Main list1:00–end-2:00Numbered items (5 to 25 depending on the title). Per item: what people do wrong → why it damages the car (simple mechanics) → what it costs in euros (with source or 'roughly') → what to do instead, step by step → a short Horst remark or story. Every 3–4 items: a mini re-hook ('Und Nummer 9 hat mir mal einen Motor gerettet…').
Merke dirlast 1:30Short summary of the 3 most important points, safety reminder when relevant (brakes, steering, tires, electrics → workshop).
Endinglast 0:30Call to action (comment your car), mention the Werkstatt-Handbuch only if the link exists, fixed ending line.

Comment écrire le script

  • Write in German, 'du' form, short spoken sentences (8–15 words). No bullet-point language in the voice text.
  • Start from the reference video (link in the video list): get its transcript, keep its structure and the kind of promise, never copy its sentences. Adapt to Germany (diesel, DSG, TÜV, road salt, Autobahn, euro prices, German brands) and add information it does not have.
  • Every number, price, law or technical claim goes into fact_check with a source (ADAC, TÜV, DEKRA, manufacturer, StVO). If no source is found, remove the claim or say 'ungefähr' / 'je nach Auto'.
  • Replace false or exaggerated words from the reference title: 'ILLEGAL', 'repairs 95 % of problems', 'forever', 'without any repair' → true versions ('ohne große Reparaturen', 'deutlich länger').
  • No medical, legal or financial advice beyond common facts. Legal points (StVO, Bußgeld) are stated as general information with the paragraph.
  • Safety: never explain work on brakes, steering, airbags, high-voltage EV systems or fuel lines as DIY. Say clearly: 'Das gehört in die Fachwerkstatt.'
  • Never accuse a named company or workshop chain. Describe practices and give the customer's rights (Kostenvoranschlag, alte Teile zurückverlangen, Rechnung prüfen).
  • Brands: talk about cars in general first. Name brands as examples, never as the main subject, except in the 'best and worst engines' format (about once every two weeks).
  • Use the running gags at most 2–3 times per video, short.
  • Use the fixed lines from character.fixed_lines_de exactly.
  • No idling advice: warming up a stationary engine is forbidden in Germany (StVO § 30) and bad for the engine.
  • Length check: count the words of all voice texts; 1,900–2,700 words.

Les B-rolls

Comment choisir la source

  1. Generic real scene (traffic, snow, gas station, car wash, Autobahn, parking, tire shop, someone driving) → BROLL_STOCK.
  2. Precise mechanical detail, damage, inside of a part, or a scene stock does not have → BROLL_AI.
  3. Anything with Horst, his workshop, his red Kadett, Gisela or Jonas → BROLL_AI using the reference images (keeps the same look).
  4. A number, a rule, a law, a list title → TEXT_CARD (never ask an AI model to write text).
  5. If a stock clip shows an American setting (US plates, pickups, US gas stations, US road signs, right-hand traffic signs in English) → reject it and search again or use AI.
Le look allemand

European cars (VW, Opel, Mercedes, BMW, Ford, Skoda shapes, badges not readable), German/EU license plates blurred or not readable, German road signs, overcast northern-European light, Ruhr-area towns and workshops. No US cars, no pickup trucks, no US gas stations.

Répartition

Roughly 50–60 % stock, 25–35 % AI, 10–15 % text cards inside the B-roll time.

Durée des clips

Stock: cut 3–8 s pieces. AI video: 3–6 s clips generated from an AI image (image-to-video).

Interdit
  • Readable brand logos or license plates
  • People's faces in close-up from stock if they could be mistaken for Horst's customers
  • Fake diagrams or text made by AI
  • Dangerous actions shown as normal (driving without seatbelt, working under a car on a jack only)

Montage et miniature

format

16:9, 1920×1080, 25 or 30 fps, H.264, audio −14 LUFS.

subtitles

German subtitles from the script (SRT). Upload the SRT to YouTube; burned-in captions optional for the first 30 s only.

music

Calm, low (−25 to −30 dB under the voice), royalty-free (YouTube Audio Library or a licensed library). No music under the first sentence.

text_cards

Bold condensed font, white on dark navy (#1f4e79) or red (#b23a2a) like Horst's workshop; item numbers big (#7); max 6 words.

sound

Light workshop sounds (wrench, engine start) on matching B-roll, low volume.

chapters

One chapter per list item; first chapter at 0:00.

Miniature

Horst's face (left, ~40 %), looking at the camera, surprised or serious; the part or problem on the right; 2–4 big German words; one red arrow or circle.

Prompt de miniature
Same man as the reference image, close-up, serious surprised expression, holding [PART] toward the camera, small German workshop background blurred, strong contrast, photorealistic, 16:9, empty space on the right for text, no text

Les étapes de production

  1. Pick the next video from publishing_order (or the one the user asks for).
  2. Inspiration: open the reference video, get its transcript, list its structure, its items and its promise. Note what is missing or wrong for Germany.
  3. Research: build a fact sheet (claims + sources ADAC/TÜV/DEKRA/manufacturer/StVO).
  4. Script: write the German script following video_structure and script_rules, output in script_output_schema.
  5. Fact-check: every fact_check entry must be 'ok' or removed. Recount words (1,900–2,700).
  6. Voice: generate audio per segment with the fixed voice; check pronunciation of numbers and abbreviations.
  7. Avatar: for every HORST_* segment, lip-sync the segment audio on the right reference image (max 15 s per clip). HORST_LAUGHS = 2–3 s image-to-video without speech.
  8. B-roll stock: search the queries, download, cut, log in credits.csv.
  9. B-roll AI: generate the images, then 3–6 s clips; check hands, faces, wheels and logos.
  10. Edit: lay the full voice track, place Horst clips and B-roll, text cards, AI label 0:00–0:05, music, sound, subtitles.
  11. Quality check (qa_checklist), export.
  12. Publishing pack: title, description, chapters, tags, pinned comment, thumbnail; tick 'altered or synthetic content' in YouTube Studio.

Contrôle avant publication

  • AI label visible 0:00–0:05 and in the description.
  • 'Altered or synthetic content' ticked in YouTube Studio.
  • No avatar clip over 15 s; no lip-sync glitch left uncovered.
  • No American setting in B-roll; no readable logo or plate.
  • Every number has a source; no false word left from the reference title.
  • Safety sentence present when brakes, tires, steering or electrics are mentioned.
  • Length 14–20 min; chapters match the items.
  • credits.csv complete for all stock clips.
  • Impressum link in the description (required in Germany, § 5 DDG).

Règles à ne jamais casser

  • EU AI Act Art. 50: AI-generated person must be disclosed (applies since 2 Aug 2026).
  • Impressum required in Germany (§ 5 DDG) before monetisation.
  • Affiliate or product links marked 'Werbung' / 'Anzeige'.
  • No fake credentials, no fake reviews, no invented statistics.
  • Do not use the face or voice of a real person for Horst.
  • If unsure about a fact, mark it 'to_verify' and ask the user instead of inventing.
